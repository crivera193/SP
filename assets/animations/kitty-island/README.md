Source: https://lottiefiles.com/free-animation/loader-cat-dark-ibvLuFJYvV
Owner supplied `Loader cat Dark.json` on October 21, 2026.
`cat-dark.json` preserves the supplied bytes. No embedded images or external assets.

License: Lottie Simple License (FL 9.13.21), included in LICENSE.txt.
Orange and gray are color-only derivatives under that same license, generated by
`python tool/create_kitty_variants.py`. Shape paths and motion keyframes are unchanged.
The source is 280 × 200, 25 fps, frames 0–32 (1.28 seconds).

Coat, rear shading and inner-ear fills/strokes can be edited separately. The pale
markings, nose and shadows remain original. `Queue` contains animated tail paths;
larger tail movement needs controlled edits to those keyframes and playback review.
Walking requires new poses, not merely moving the seated drawing across the map.
